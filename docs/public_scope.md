# Public vs Private Scope

## Public in this repository

- deterministic overlay/reference audit;
- adaptive buffer calculation;
- selected safety guardrails;
- simplified OCO preference logic;
- synthetic examples;
- regression tests;
- research documentation.

## Intentionally private

- the complete Golden Sniper / 6316 instruction pack;
- full candidate search, phase interpretation and correction path;
- complete hard-veto implementation and case-study memory;
- production-level trade management;
- real trade records until anonymised for research publication;
- broker integration and account data;
- prompts and private AI conversations.

The goal is to demonstrate system-design and validation capability without turning the portfolio repository into a deployable live trading strategy.
